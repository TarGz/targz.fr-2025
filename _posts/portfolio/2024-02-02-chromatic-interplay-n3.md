Four pen passes, four ink colors. The parallel pen turned to a different angle each pass. The overlap makes a moiré.

Number 3 in the Chromatic Interplay series. Two of the passes use the same Ecoline Pastel Roser, once at 45° and once at 135°. Chartreuse and Turquoise Green fill in between.

Home made generative truchet tilling algorythm.
